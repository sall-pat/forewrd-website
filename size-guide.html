How to Measure

Bust — Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level.

Waist — Measure the narrowest part of your natural waistline.

Hips — Measure around the widest part of your hips.

Shoulder — Measure from shoulder seam to shoulder seam across the back.

Between sizes or unsure? Don't worry — since every piece is made-to-order, we can adjust to your exact measurements. Just reach out and we'll guide you.
