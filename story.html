{ Our Story }

{

FOREWRD Studio is a contemporary label crafting made-to-order clothing and fine jewellery. Born from a love for fashion as play, as character building, as the quiet confidence of showing up exactly as you intended.

We design for the women who see getting dressed as an act of self-expression — not obligation. Women in their mid-twenties to late forties who understand that what you wear is the opening line to every conversation you'll have that day.

"Dress with intention, express without restraint, show up confidently — slipping into character, always for the plot."

Every piece is made-to-order. We don't mass produce. We don't do fast fashion. Each garment is cut, stitched, and finished for one person — you. Our fine jewellery follows the same principle: 22kt gold, crafted with purpose, built to stay.

}

What We Stand For

Intention

Every design decision is deliberate. Structure meets ease. Drama meets restraint.

Craft

Made-to-order means no waste, no compromise. Each piece is built for the person wearing it.

Expression

Fashion should be playful, theatrical, and unapologetically yours.
